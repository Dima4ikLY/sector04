# Audio sources

## Weapon shots

`weapon-shots.wav` uses recordings by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, and Matthew Nanney. The creators released [The Free Firearm Sound Library](https://web.archive.org/web/20141217041948/http://freefirearmsfx.com/) under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The source files are from the [Prepared SFX archive mirror](https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary/tree/beb2f4041f3d6740fa0aeaf0e71159bd65a78c1b/Prepared%20SFX).

The bank is a mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV with two 400 ms slots per weapon, in engine weapon order. Each take is trimmed to a single shot, equalized, level matched, and faded before the next slot. RX-9 also uses a short synthesized electrical tail.

## Doors and lifts

`mechanisms.wav` combines synthesized motor and braking sounds with edited recordings released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The sources are `elevator_door.wav` by [PagDev](https://opengameart.org/content/elevatordoor) and `clank1.wav` and `lightclunk1.wav` by [Brian MacIntosh (BMacZero)](https://opengameart.org/content/mechanical-sounds).

The bank is a mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV with four 800 ms slots for door opening, door closing, lift starting, and lift stopping in playback order.

## Footsteps

`footsteps.wav` uses trimmed and level-matched takes from the published previews of three [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) recordings. The bank is a mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV with three 320 ms takes per material, in the order below.

- Metal grate. [Metal Footsteps by SoftDistortionFX](https://freesound.org/people/SoftDistortionFX/sounds/398937/)
- Concrete. [concrete_17a_boots_walk.wav by sturmankin](https://freesound.org/people/sturmankin/sounds/272253/)
- Rock. [Footsteps Mountain Boots Rock Walk Sequence by Nox_Sound](https://freesound.org/people/Nox_Sound/sounds/558472/)

## Jumps and landings

`jumps-landings.wav` combines edited recordings from the published [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) previews below with the metal and concrete layers from the footstep sources. The bank is a mono, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Three 440 ms takeoffs in metal, concrete, and stone order are followed by three landings in the same material order.

- Rock takeoff and landing. [Mountain Boots Rock Jump Sequence by Nox_Sound](https://freesound.org/people/Nox_Sound/sounds/558477/)
- Hard surface landing. [Jumping onto a hard floor with shoes by wibwob](https://freesound.org/people/wibwob/sounds/277717/)
- Heavy impact. [jump.wav by pcmac](https://freesound.org/people/pcmac/sounds/464527/)
- Shoe scuff on concrete. [Feet Draging against concrete.wav by Chubbers1995](https://freesound.org/people/Chubbers1995/sounds/344626/)

## Bullet impacts

`bullet-impacts.wav` combines edited [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) Foley from the published previews below with synthesized impact onsets. The mono PCM16 bank uses 44.1 kHz and nine 300 ms slots. It contains three takes per material, in metal, concrete, and stone order.

- Metal pipe. [Metal Hits by Bricklover](https://freesound.org/people/Bricklover/sounds/532885/)
- Concrete wall. [Short pop by newagesoup](https://freesound.org/people/newagesoup/sounds/369136/) · [Short thump by newagesoup](https://freesound.org/people/newagesoup/sounds/369123/) · [Dry hit by newagesoup](https://freesound.org/people/newagesoup/sounds/369079/)
- Stone. [Foley Rocks Stones Impacts by Nox_Sound](https://freesound.org/people/Nox_Sound/sounds/567701/)

## Bullet flybys

`bullet-flybys.wav` contains three original synthesized takes built from a brief pressure crack and a descending air rush. The mono PCM16 bank uses 44.1 kHz and three 180 ms slots.

## Grenade explosions

`grenade-explosions.wav` contains three original synthesized takes. Each layers a sharp crack and low thump with short reflections and falling debris. The mono PCM16 bank uses 44.1 kHz and three 1.2 s slots.

## Grenade bounces

`grenade-bounces.wav` contains original synthesized impacts of a grenade casing on metal, concrete, and stone. The mono PCM16 bank uses 44.1 kHz and nine 320 ms slots with three takes per material, in that order.

## Grenade throws

`grenade-throws.wav` contains three original synthesized takes that pair a textured metallic pin pull with a separate lever snap. The mono PCM16 bank uses 44.1 kHz and three 360 ms slots.

## Weapon reloads

`weapon-reloads.wav` contains original synthesized Foley in twelve 280 ms mono PCM16 slots at 44.1 kHz. The first nine slots contain three takes each of magazine removal, insertion and the final bolt click. The last three slots contain the BR-8 launcher's opening, loading and closing sounds.

## Weapon switches

`weapon-switches.wav` contains original synthesized Foley in six 400 ms mono PCM16 slots at 44.1 kHz. Each pairs a cloth-and-gear holstering sound with a separate draw. The first three takes are for pistols and the last three are for heavier weapons.

## Player impacts

`player-hits.wav` contains original synthesized impact Foley for body, armor and helmet contacts. The mono PCM16 bank uses 44.1 kHz with three 300 ms takes per material in that order.

## Body falls

`death-falls.wav` contains original synthesized body-fall and gear Foley. The mono PCM16 bank uses 44.1 kHz and nine 480 ms slots, three takes each for metal, concrete and stone.

## Respawns

`respawns.wav` contains three original synthesized takes that combine a short boot plant with clothing and equipment sounds. The mono PCM16 bank uses 44.1 kHz and three 400 ms slots.

## Slides

`slides.wav` contains original synthesized slides with a quick fabric and boot onset, sustained surface friction and a tapered release. The mono PCM16 bank uses 44.1 kHz and nine 560 ms slots, three each for metal, concrete and stone.
